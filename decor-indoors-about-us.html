About Decor Indoors

A Studio Built On Listening.

Decor Indoors is a full-service interior design studio based in Mangalore, creating thoughtful residential and commercial spaces that feel personal, functional, and timeless. For over 12 years, we have worked with homeowners, businesses, and hospitality clients to transform ordinary rooms into environments that truly reflect the people who use them. We believe good design is not about following trends — it is about understanding how you live, work, and move through a space, then shaping every detail around that rhythm.

From complete home interiors and renovation to furniture styling and design consultation, our work brings together warm materials, intelligent planning, and refined craftsmanship. With 250+ projects completed and 180+ happy clients, our focus remains simple: listen carefully, design honestly, and deliver spaces that feel as good as they look.

Our Approach

Collaborative from the first conversation.

Our approach is collaborative from the very first conversation. We begin by listening — to your ideas, your routines, your budget, and the way you want your space to feel. From there, we plan, design, refine, execute, and deliver with clarity and care. Whether it is a family home, a retail store, an office, or a hospitality project, we manage the journey end-to-end so you can enjoy the process without the usual stress.

Rooted in Mangalore and working across India and beyond, we combine local craftsmanship with a global design perspective. Every project is guided by three beliefs: spaces should serve real life, details should be intentional, and good design should last. The result is interiors that do more than look beautiful — they support better living, better work, and better experiences.

Our Vision

To be a trusted interior design studio known for creating timeless, functional, and deeply personal spaces that elevate everyday living and working — rooted in Mangalore, recognised across India and beyond.

Our Mission

Our mission is to design with empathy, plan with precision, and deliver with integrity.
