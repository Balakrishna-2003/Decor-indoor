Design Consultation

Honest guidance before the first line is drawn.

A consultation is where good design begins. It is a chance to slow down, look at your space with fresh eyes, and get honest direction on layout, materials and budget — before you commit to anything. It saves time, money and the regrets that come from guessing.

We walk through your home or office with you, listen to how you actually use the space, and put together a clear, practical roadmap you can take forward — with us or on your own. No jargon, no pressure, no hidden costs.
