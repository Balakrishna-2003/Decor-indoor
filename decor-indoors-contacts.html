Common Questions

Before you write to us.

A few things most clients ask us before starting a project. Still curious? Write to us at info@decorindoors.com.

How do I begin a project with Decor Indoors?

It starts with a conversation. Send us a message or give us a call — we'll schedule a discovery call to understand your space, your needs, and your budget. From there we prepare a proposal and a design roadmap.

What is the typical timeline for a project?

A single-room interior typically takes 4–6 weeks, a full home interior 10–16 weeks, and larger commercial projects 12–24 weeks. The exact timeline is confirmed during the design phase.

Do you work outside of Mangalore?

Yes. We are based in Mangalore but have delivered projects across India and in the Middle East. Remote projects are handled with dedicated site supervision and regular progress updates.

Can you work with my existing furniture?

Absolutely. We often integrate meaningful existing pieces into new designs. During the concept phase we'll review what you already own and plan around it.

Do you offer turnkey interiors?

Yes — most of our projects are turnkey. Design, procurement, execution and styling are all managed under one roof, so you can hand over the keys and step into a finished space.

We design and create inspiring spaces that bring together beauty, functionality and a better way of living.
